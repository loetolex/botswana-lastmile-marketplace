# Gaborone demo storefront proof of concept

Loeto Go Phase 3 includes 50 demo storefronts based on current public Gaborone restaurant listings.

Important: these are discovery/proof-of-concept listings, not claims that the restaurants are partnered with Loeto Go.

## Public research sources used

- Tripadvisor Gaborone restaurant directory and category pages
- Wanderlog Gaborone restaurant roundups
- EatOutMap 2026 Gaborone listings
- Zen Cafe Lounge public menu
- House of Briscoe public menus for The Daily Grind and Two Six Seven
- Nando's Botswana public menu
- Casa del Sol public site/menu
- Public business listings for specific venues

## Menu policy

- Import actual dish names/prices only where a public menu source is available.
- Show a source link on the storefront.
- Do not invent menu prices.
- Storefronts without a sourced menu remain discovery-only until a public menu is imported or a merchant claims the listing.

## Visual policy

The Loeto Go app uses its own visual identity. Restaurant cards use colorful category/brand-inspired treatments and food photography for proof of concept; they are not presented as official restaurant apps or endorsements.
